ontology-dynamics-epistemology

Q: isn't virtually everything about

ontology → dynamics → epistemology?

Yes—if by that you mean:
what is → how it changes / behaves → what can be known about it

That triad shows up almost everywhere.

Ontology
What exists; the state space
  • What are the "things"?
  • What degrees of freedom exist?
  • What distinctions matter?
Dynamics
Rules of transformation
  • What interacts with what?
  • What are the update rules?
  • What generates trajectories?
Epistemology
What an observer can infer
  • What is observable?
  • What is identifiable?
  • What remains hidden?

That's basically:

xt+1 = f(xt) ontology → the space x lives in dynamics → the function f epistemology → infer x or f from observations

The same structure, elsewhere

Bayesian
prior structure → generative process → posterior belief
Physics
configuration space → equations of motion → measurement
Personal identity
"what am i?" → "how do i change?" → "how do i know?"

Ukubona tensor language

Scalar / Action→ontological commitment — "something is there"
Vector / Intention→directionality / dynamic force
Matrix / Attention→selective observation
Tensor / Sensation→structured evidence
Eigenmode / Reaction→stabilized knowledge / belief

Even philosophy often secretly reduces to this:

Parmenides
obsessed over ontology
"what is"
Heraclitus
obsessed over dynamics
"everything flows"
Kant
obsessed over epistemology
"what can be known"

And the modern synthesis is: you can't separate them.

  • Your ontology constrains your dynamics think only particles exist → write particle equations; think fields exist → write field equations
  • Your dynamics constrains your epistemology chaotic systems foreclose long-term prediction; reversible systems permit retrodiction; noisy systems limit inference
  • Your epistemology feeds back into ontology what you can measure determines what you claim exists — wave function vs. hidden variables
Ontology  ↔  Dynamics  ↔  Epistemology
a closed loop, not a chain

That's why control theory, neuroscience, politics, theology, and personal identity all rhyme.
That may be the universal compression.
